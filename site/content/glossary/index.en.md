Terms that appear on the species pages, listed in Japanese syllabary (gojūon) order of their Japanese readings.

## F1, F2 {#f1-f2}

Names for generations born in captivity. The offspring of two wild-caught parents (wild fish) are F1; the offspring of two F1 fish are F2.

## Data from a related species {#related-species-data}

Numbers or observations recorded not for the species itself but for a closely related fish. On this site, the text says "data from a related species" to keep these separate from records of the species itself.

## Satellite {#satellite}

A small breeding box hung on the outside of an aquarium. It draws in water from the parents' tank, so eggs and fry can be raised separately.

## CW number {#cw-number}

A reference number that Corydoras World, a project of researchers and hobbyists, assigns to Corydoras and their relatives that have no scientific name. For example, Corydoras "Zebrina" is CW111 (["2. Identity" on the Zebrina page](/aqua-species-note/en/species/corydoras-cw111-zebrina/#identity)).

## GH, TDS {#gh-tds}

Rough measures of water quality. GH (general hardness) is the amount of calcium and magnesium dissolved in the water, and TDS is the total amount of everything dissolved in it. For both, the lower the value, the "softer" the water.

## (inference) {#inference}

Not a record itself, but something the author has worked out from the records. On this site, "(inference)" is written at the end of the sentence.

## T-position {#t-position}

The posture Corydoras and their relatives take when spawning. The female brings her mouth to around the male's belly, and seen from above the 2 fish form the shape of a T.

## Blackwater, clearwater {#blackwater-clearwater}

A way of classifying the water of South American rivers. Blackwater is brown from substances dissolved out of fallen leaves and the like, and leans acidic. Clearwater is highly transparent water.

## Bred (captive-bred) {#bred}

Fish born and raised in captivity. Fish bred in Japan are called "kokusan breed" (Japan-bred).

## (unverified) {#unverified}

Information whose original source could not be opened and which has not been confirmed. The text marks it as "(unverified)".

## Undescribed species {#undescribed}

A species that has not yet been formally described in a paper and has no scientific name.

## (needs checking) {#to-be-checked}

Information that may have been misheard or miswritten, such as numbers picked up from YouTube auto-captions or hearsay.

## Yolk sac {#yolk-sac}

The sac of nutrients attached to the belly of newly hatched fry. Around the time it is used up, the fry start to eat.

## Wild {#wild}

Fish collected from the wild.
