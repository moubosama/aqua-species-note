How to read the numbers

Every page uses the same marks

  • ［n］Source number. Tap it to jump to the references at the end of the page.
  • VerifiedA record checked against the original source.
  • Needs checkingA number picked up from subtitles or hearsay. It may have been misheard.
  • UnverifiedThe original source could not be opened, so it has not been checked.

HOW WE WRITE

How these notes are made

  1. 1

    Collect records from around the world

    Taxonomic papers, breeding diaries on overseas forums, Japanese blogs and shop arrival records.

  2. 2

    Check against the originals

    Numbers are checked in the original sources, and how far they were checked is shown as Verified, Needs checking or Unverified.

  3. 3

    Summarize in our own words

    No text or photos are copied. What is not known is written as not known.
